Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-18

This Privacy Policy describes how the internal advertising management application test01 ("the Application") handles information. The Application is a private, single-operator tool used to create, review and manage advertising campaigns on Meta platforms on behalf of its operator's own advertising accounts. It is not offered as a public service and does not onboard external users.

1. Information We Access

The Application accesses the following categories of information, solely through Meta's official APIs and only after the operator has explicitly granted permission:

  • Advertising account data — account ID, status, currency, time zone, and campaign / ad set / ad configuration belonging to the operator.
  • Ad creative content — the text, images, links and calls to action that the operator submits for review.
  • Ad review results — approval status and policy feedback returned by Meta for submitted creatives.
  • Page information — the ID and name of Facebook Pages owned by the operator, used only to attach ads to the correct Page.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal data of end users, ad viewers, or any third parties.
  • We do not collect passwords, payment card numbers, or government identifiers.
  • We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics on this page.
  • We do not access private messages, friend lists, or any social graph data.

3. How Information Is Used

Accessed information is used exclusively to:

  • Create and configure advertising campaigns requested by the operator.
  • Retrieve and display advertising review results so the operator can correct policy issues before publishing.
  • Maintain an internal log of review outcomes for the operator's own quality-control purposes.

No information is used for profiling, advertising targeting of third parties, or any purpose unrelated to the operator's own campaigns.

4. Data Storage and Retention

All data is stored locally on the operator's own computer. Advertising metadata and review results are retained only as long as needed for the operator's internal records, and are deleted on request. No data is uploaded to any third-party server operated by us.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose any accessed information to third parties. Data is transmitted only to Meta's own APIs as required to perform the operations described above.

6. Security

Access credentials are stored locally and are never transmitted to any server other than Meta's official endpoints. Access tokens can be revoked at any time through Meta Business Settings, which immediately terminates the Application's access.

7. Your Rights and Control

Because the Application operates on the operator's own accounts, the operator retains full control at all times and may:

  • Revoke the Application's access via Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites, or via Business Settings → System Users.
  • Request deletion of any locally stored data.
  • Discontinue use at any time without notice.

8. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to anyone under the age of 18 and does not knowingly process data relating to minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

Any material change to this policy will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
